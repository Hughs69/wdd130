Los Dientes del Diablo

Extreme - 8 hours
Our most challenging run through narrow canyons and Class IV-V rapids. Includes lunch and professional photos. Perfect for thrill-seekers with previous experience.

Big Mallard

Intermediate - 6 hours
A perfect balance of excitement and scenery. Class III-IV rapids with calm stretches for wildlife viewing. Great for groups and families with older kids (12+).

Half Day Adventure

Beginner - 3 hours
Ideal for first-timers and families with young children. Gentle Class I-II rapids, safety briefing, and all equipment provided. Morning and afternoon departures.
