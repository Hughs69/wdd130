Ghana

Tema is a city on the Bight of Benin and Atlantic coast of Ghana. It is located 25 kilometres (16 mi) east of the capital city, Accra, in the Greater Accra Region, and is the capital of the Tema Metropolitan District. As of 2013, Tema was the eleventh most populous settlement in Ghana, with a population of approximately 161,612 people, a marked decrease from its 2005 population of 209,000.
